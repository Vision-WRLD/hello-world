Privacy Policy

How Vision WRLD collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Vision WRLD ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at hellovisionwrld@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit the contact form we collect your name, business name, email address, optional phone number, your message, and the budget you select. If you chat with our ATLAS assistant, we process the messages you type.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This is used for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To provide and improve our services and this website.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the site. They only process your data to provide their service to us:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and powers the ATLAS assistant. See their privacy policy at cloudflare.com.
  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox. See their privacy policy at web3forms.com.
  • Google provides the email inbox that receives your messages. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not use third-party analytics. Our hosting provider may set essential cookies that are necessary for security and performance.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and, where relevant, to deliver a project, after which we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email hellovisionwrld@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep your data safe.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
